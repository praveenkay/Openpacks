What We Fix First

Researched pain points that cost the most money or time. Packs prioritize these for the new focus verticals.

Property / Rentals

Late rent & maintenance chaos

Rent reconciliation can take 12–15 hrs/week. Requests by text with no tracking. Owner statements go out late. Spreadsheets everywhere.

HVAC / Trades

Scheduling waste & delayed invoices

Manual routes waste $3k–$5k/month. Paper tickets sit for days. Techs arrive without parts. Dispatchers burn ~15 hrs/week.

Landscaping

Route chaos & recurring billing gaps

Wasted drive time across crews. Invoices delayed days/weeks. Recurring contracts missed or inconsistently billed. Weather changes poorly communicated.

Handyman

Admin longer than the job

Quoting and invoicing take longer than small jobs. Missed materials markup and scope creep. Rare same-day invoicing. Disconnected tools.

Small Vendors

Offline checkout & perishable waste

Markets need offline-first POS. Fast checkout in tight spaces. Stockouts or waste from poor tracking. Limited real-time visibility.

Retail

Stockouts at the counter

56% discover stockouts only when the customer is there. Disconnected POS and inventory. Manual counts waste hours.

Restaurants

Table & kitchen blindness

Nobody knows which table is free. Paper tickets jam or arrive late. Slow turnover and inventory guesswork.

General SMB

Spreadsheet invoicing

Quotes, invoices, and payments live in disconnected tools. Late payments invisible. Limited reporting.

Competitive Landscape

How OpenPacks undercuts typical SaaS pricing while using real open-source bases.

Property

vs AppFolio / Buildium

AppFolio ~$1.40+/unit with 50-unit min. Buildium from ~$62/mo. OpenProperty free self-host covers units, leases, rent ledger, work orders. Paid adds portal, auto-reminders, owner statements.

Field Service

vs Jobber / Housecall Pro

Jobber from ~$29–49/mo, Housecall Pro from ~$59–79/mo (seat-based, higher for teams). OpenFieldPro / OpenFieldService free covers jobs, scheduling, invoicing. Paid adds GPS, same-day invoicing, routes.

Custom AI

vs Afrex-style builds

Custom AI/ops platforms often start $7.5k–$10k+. OpenPacks starter packs $499–$2,500 one-time + managed $49–$249/mo deliver similar pain-point fixes at SMB prices.
